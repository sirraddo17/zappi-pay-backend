======================================================================
ZAPPI PAY: CUSTOMER FEATURE FACT SHEET (for the in-app AI assistant)
======================================================================

HOW TO READ THIS
- "Home" is the main screen. The bottom bar has four tabs: Home, Wallet, Orders and Profile.
- Home shows a "Services" grid with these tiles: Airtime, Data, Electricity, Cable TV, Education, Internet, Bet Funding and More. A "Send Money" tile also shows, but only while sending money to users or to banks is switched on.
- Home → More ("More services") has these tiles: Airtime to Cash, Print Cards, Bulk airtime & data, Best data deals, Refer & Earn and Family wallet.
- A floating Help bubble (button labelled "Open help", window titled "ZappiPay Help") appears on every logged-in screen. It is hidden on the login, signup, password, legal and admin pages.
- "Set by ZAPPI PAY (currently configurable)" means an admin setting. The value in brackets is the code DEFAULT, not necessarily today's live value.
- Every purchase and transfer needs the 4-digit transaction PIN, or fingerprint / Face ID on a device where that is set up.

0. FEATURES IN THE BACKEND WITH NO (OR ONLY A PARTIAL) CUSTOMER SCREEN
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Rewards split. This runs in the background. Customers only see "🎁 This purchase earns you cashback and points" on the Buy screen.
- Reward safety limit [rewardGuardEnabled, rewardGuardPercent]. This runs in the background. It can quietly reduce a promo discount, cashback, points or shop commission on a purchase.
- Fraud hold on bank transfers. Customers only see a "Transfer Under Review" notification.
- Email alerts are sent only when the admin has switched on emailAlertsEnabled (default off). The customer toggle is at Profile → "Email alerts".
- Low-balance warning before a scheduled top-up. This arrives as a notification only.
- Escalations ("Report to VTpass"). These are for admins only.

1. WALLET FUNDING
----------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT IT IS
You add money to your ZAPPI PAY wallet and pay for everything from it. The ways (each can be switched on or off by ZAPPI PAY):
- Automatic funding: you get your own personal bank account number, through ZAPPI PAY's payment partner Monnify [setting: monnifyFundingEnabled, default ON].
- Card or USSD: pay with a debit card (Verve, Mastercard, Visa) or your bank's USSD code on Flutterwave's secure page; added instantly [setting: flutterwaveEnabled, default OFF].
- Manual funding: you pay into a ZAPPI PAY business account, then an admin approves it.

BENEFITS
- A personal account number credits your wallet automatically, with no waiting for approval.
- You can transfer from any bank app.
- A calculator tells you exactly how much to send when a fee applies.
- Manual funding is a backup when the bank network is having problems.

HOW TO GET STARTED: PERSONAL ACCOUNT NUMBER
1. Tap Wallet (bottom bar), or tap "Fund Wallet" on the Home balance card.
2. Find the card "Get your personal account number". Choose "BVN" or "NIN" and enter the 11 digits.
3. Tap "Get My Account Number". This is a one-time setup.
4. Copy one of the account numbers shown under "Fund by bank transfer" using its "Copy" button, and transfer to it from any bank.
5. If the money hasn't shown up yet, tap "I've sent money — check now".

HOW TO GET STARTED: CARD OR USSD
1. Wallet → the card "💳 Pay with card or USSD".
2. Enter "Amount to add (₦)" and tap "Pay ₦X" (X includes the card processing fee, if any).
3. You go to Flutterwave's secure page: pay by card or USSD. ZAPPI PAY never sees your card details.
4. You come back to Wallet; it says "₦X added to your wallet". If it says "Still confirming", it is added automatically within a few minutes (you get a notification). If you didn't finish, nothing is taken.
- Card fee: set by ZAPPI PAY [setting: cardFundingFeePercent, default 0%] [setting: cardFundingFeeCap, default ₦0 = no cap], added on top — the wallet gets the full amount you typed. Minimum = the funding minimum; maximum ₦500,000 per payment.
- If a method is switched off, its card disappears from Wallet. Money already sent to a personal account number is still credited even when bank-transfer funding is off.

HOW TO GET STARTED: MANUAL FUNDING
1. Go to Wallet. Use the "Fund Wallet" form, or, if you already have a personal account, open "Other way: send to our business account for manual approval".
2. Transfer to the business account shown. If more than one is shown, tick the one you used.
3. Enter "Amount (₦)", and optionally "Transfer reference" and "Note". Tap "Submit Funding Request".

RULES, LIMITS AND FEES
- BVN or NIN must be exactly 11 digits.
- Name and date of birth check [idMatchEnabled, default OFF]: when on, the customer's name (as in Profile) and date of birth must match their BVN/NIN before the account number is given. They enter their date of birth and tick a consent box on the same card. Up to 3 tries a day. If it says the name doesn't match, they fix the spelling in Profile (first name and surname as on the BVN/NIN) and try again; if the date of birth doesn't match, they enter the one on their BVN/NIN. After a match, the name and date of birth are locked — only support can change them.
- One BVN or NIN can only be linked to one ZAPPI PAY account. If it is already in use, the customer sees "already linked to another ZAPPI PAY account… contact support".
- ZAPPI PAY says it does not store the BVN or NIN; it is sent only to the payment partner. (The code keeps only a scrambled one-way check, not the number itself.)
- Automatic funding fee: a percentage of each transfer, with a cap.
  - Set by ZAPPI PAY (currently configurable) [setting: bankFundingFeePercent, default 0%] [setting: bankFundingFeeCap, default ₦0 = no cap]. Both at 0 means free.
  - The fee is taken from the amount received. The wallet gets the amount minus the fee, and the notification shows the fee.
  - When a fee applies, the Wallet page shows "How much do you want in your wallet?" and tells you to "Send exactly ₦X".
  - When the fee matches the partner's own charge, the app says it goes to the payment partner and "ZAPPI PAY does not keep it".
- There is no minimum for automatic funding.
- Manual funding minimum: set by ZAPPI PAY (currently configurable) [setting: minFundingAmount, default ₦100].
- Manual funding requests stay "PENDING" until an admin approves them. The customer then gets "Wallet Funded" or "Funding Request Rejected".
- The "check now" button can be tapped once every 15 seconds.
- The same bank payment can never be credited twice.
- If a bank is having network problems, the admin can hide that account number [setting: hiddenFundingBanks]. The app then says "temporarily unavailable because of bank network issues" and points to the business account.

ADMIN SWITCHES
- Automatic funding only shows when Monnify is set up.
- Manual funding: [manualFundingEnabled, default on], plus a separate on/off switch for each business account [manualAccounts].

COMMON QUESTIONS
- "I sent money but it's not in my wallet." Tap "I've sent money — check now" on Wallet. Most transfers arrive within minutes, and some banks take up to 30 minutes. If it still hasn't arrived, message support (Profile → Support → "💬 Message support in the app") with a screenshot of the bank alert. Money sent to your account number is not lost.
- "Why do you need my BVN/NIN?" Banks require it to create a personal account number.
- "Why did I get less than I sent?" The bank processing fee applies (see above). Use the "Send exactly" calculator next time.

Coupon codes ("🎟️ Have a coupon code?" on Wallet) are covered in section 23 (Promo codes).

2. HOW ALL PURCHASES WORK (airtime, data, electricity, cable TV, education, internet, bet funding)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
These rules apply to every service in sections 3 to 9.

HOW TO BUY
1. Home → tap the service tile.
2. Choose the provider, enter the number, and choose a plan or type an amount.
3. Check the "Total". Optionally tap "Have a promo code?".
4. Tap "Pay ₦X".
5. Confirm with your PIN or fingerprint in the "Confirm payment" sheet.

PRICE
- The price is the supplier's price, plus any ZAPPI PAY markup, minus any discount.
  - Markup: [setting: markupPercentByService, default none = 0%], capped by [markupCapByService].
  - Discount: [setting: discountPercentByService, default none].
- When there is a discount, the Home tile shows an "X% OFF" badge and the Buy screen shows "🎉 X% off all … purchases right now".
- The server re-checks the price when you pay.

MINIMUM
- For services where you type an amount (airtime, electricity, bet funding), the minimum is set by ZAPPI PAY (currently configurable) [setting: minPurchaseAmount, default ₦50].
- Plan services (data, cable, education, internet) use the plan's fixed price.

CHECKS BEFORE YOU ARE CHARGED
- Your wallet balance must cover it ("Insufficient wallet balance.").
- The KYC daily limit, if switched on (section 30).
- Family limits, if you are a managed family member (section 15).
- Maintenance pauses (section 40).
- The account must not be deactivated or frozen.

PIN
- Wrong PIN: "Wrong PIN. X attempts left."
- After 5 wrong tries, the PIN locks for 15 minutes. Logging in with your password unlocks it.

FAILURE AND REFUND
- The wallet is charged first. If the purchase definitely fails, the full amount goes back automatically and the customer gets "Purchase Failed … refunded".
- If the result is unclear, the order shows as pending ("Your purchase is processing…"). It is re-checked after about 20 seconds, 2 minutes and 10 minutes, then every 15 minutes. It then either succeeds or is refunded.
- If the network is slow (over about 6 seconds), the app says you can leave the page and that you'll be refunded automatically if it fails.

AFTER SUCCESS
- You get a "Purchase Successful" notification, including "You saved ₦X" when a discount applied.
- Depending on what is switched on, you may also get cashback, loyalty points, referral bonus progress for whoever invited you, challenge progress and renewal reminders.

OPTIONAL TICKBOXES ON EVERY BUY SCREEN
- "Save this … for next time"
- "Repeat this purchase automatically" (section 26)
- For airtime and data sent to someone else's number: "🎁 Send as a gift with a message" (section 13)

----------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT IT IS: Pay for prepaid or postpaid electricity for any distribution company (disco) available on the list.

BENEFITS
- The meter name is checked before you pay.
- The token is shown on the receipt and can be copied.

HOW TO GET STARTED

WHERE IS THE TOKEN?

COMMON QUESTIONS

10. SEND MONEY: TO ZAPPI PAY USERS (P2P, by phone, username or QR)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
AVAILABILITY: switched in Admin → New features (Off / Testers only / On for everyone) [p2pEnabled, default OFF]. When it is off, customers don't see it and the app says it isn't available yet — don't tell customers how to use it.
WHAT IT IS: Instant, free wallet-to-wallet transfers to another ZAPPI PAY customer.

BENEFITS
- Instant.
- No fee in the code.
- You see the receiver's name before you send.
- Works by phone number, username, QR code or pay link.

HOW TO GET STARTED
1. Home → "Send Money" → tab "ZappiPay User".
2. Enter the "Recipient's phone number or username" and tap "Continue". The name shows under "Sending to". Use "Change" to pick someone else.
3. Enter the "Amount (₦)" and an optional "Note". Tap "Send Money" and enter your PIN.

QR CODES AND PAY LINKS
- To pay someone by QR, tap "📷 Scan QR to pay" on the same screen.
- To get paid, show "My ZAPPI PAY QR" (next to the scan button, or on Refer & Earn) and use "Save / share".
- A pay link has the form zappipay.com.ng/pay/<username>. It opens Send Money with that person filled in. For someone without an account, it opens signup with the username as their referral code.

RULES
- You can't send to yourself.
- The balance must cover the amount.
- Counts toward the KYC daily limit (section 30).
- Family members who aren't allowed to send money can only send back to the parent who manages them.
- There is no minimum or maximum in the code beyond the balance and the daily limit.
- Both sides get notifications ("Money Received" / "Money Sent").

COMMON QUESTIONS
- "Can I reverse it?" There is no reversal feature. Contact support.
- "User not found." Check the phone number or username. The receiver must have a ZAPPI PAY account.

11. AIRTIME TO CASH
----------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT IT IS: Bought airtime by mistake? Transfer it to ZAPPI PAY's line and get wallet cash back, minus a fee. A person checks it before you are paid.

BENEFITS
- Rescues airtime bought by mistake.
- The payout is shown before you submit.

HOW TO GET STARTED
1. Home → More → "Airtime to Cash". Or open an airtime receipt in Orders and tap "Bought by mistake? Convert to cash".
2. Optionally choose "Which purchase was a mistake?".
3. Choose the "Network" and enter the "Phone number the airtime is on" (11 digits starting with 0) and the "Airtime amount (₦)".
4. Check the summary: "Airtime value", "Service fee (X%)" and "You'll receive".
5. Tap "Submit Request".
6. Then send the airtime from that line to the number shown, using your network's Share & Sell or transfer option.

RULES
- Fee: set by ZAPPI PAY (currently configurable) [setting: airtimeToCashFeePercent, default 20%]. The payout is rounded down to whole naira.
- Minimum: [setting: airtimeToCashMinAmount, default ₦500]. Maximum: ₦50,000 (fixed in the code).
- Only networks with a receiving line set up can be chosen [setting: airtimeToCashNumbers]. Supported networks are MTN, Glo, Airtel and 9mobile.
- At most 3 requests can be waiting at a time.
- The admin can approve a smaller "received" amount if less airtime arrived. The payout is then recalculated.

ADMIN SWITCH: [airtimeToCashEnabled, default OFF]. When it is off, the screen says "Airtime to Cash isn't available right now."

COMMON QUESTIONS
- "When do I get paid?" After ZAPPI PAY confirms the airtime arrived. You'll get a notification.

----------------------------------------------------------------------

RULES
- Time: [setting: deliveryPromiseSeconds, default 60 seconds].

15. FAMILY WALLET
----------------------------------------------------------------------
AVAILABILITY: switched in Admin → New features (Off / Testers only / On for everyone) [familyEnabled, default OFF]. When it is off, customers don't see it and the app says it isn't available yet — don't tell customers how to use it.
WHAT IT IS: A parent links a family member's ZAPPI PAY account. The parent can then send an automatic allowance and set controls: a daily spending limit, which services the member can buy, and whether they can send money out.

BENEFITS
- Automatic weekly or monthly pocket money.
- Spending limits.
- The parent can see the member's balance, spending today and last 5 purchases.

HOW TO GET STARTED (PARENT)
1. Open Home → More → "Family wallet", or Profile → the "👨‍👩‍👧 Family" card.
2. Under "Add a family member", enter "Their username or phone number" and an optional "Nickname". Tap "Send invite".
3. Once they accept, tap "Limits & allowance" on their card and set:
   - "Allowance (₦)" and "How often" (Every week / Every month)
   - "Daily spending limit (₦)" (leave empty for no limit)
   - "They can buy:" (tick the services)
   - "Allow sending money to friends or banks"
4. Tap "Save settings".
5. "Send now" pays this period's allowance immediately. "Remove from family" ends the link.

HOW TO ACCEPT (MEMBER)
- You get a "Family Invite" notification. Open the Family screen and tap "Accept" or "Decline".
- To leave later, tap "Leave family".

RULES
- A parent can have up to 10 members.
- An account can belong to only one family.
- A managed member can't add others.
- You can't add yourself.
- Allowance: ₦50 to ₦1,000,000, weekly or monthly. It is paid from the parent's wallet.
- If the parent's wallet is short, the allowance is not sent and the parent gets "Allowance Not Sent". Funding the wallet and tapping "Send now" fixes it.
- Allowances are checked hourly.
- Daily limit: at least ₦50, or empty for none. It counts the member's successful and pending purchases today, Lagos time. Print Cards count as Airtime.
- "Allow sending money to friends or banks" is OFF by default. When it is off, the member can still send money back to the parent.
- No PIN is needed to save family settings or for "Send now".
- Either side can end the link at any time. Limits and allowance stop immediately.

ADMIN SWITCH: None.

COMMON QUESTIONS
- "My purchase was blocked." Your family settings don't allow that service, or you've reached the daily limit. Ask the family member who manages your account to change it.

17. BILL AND RENEWAL REMINDERS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT IT IS: Automatic warnings before your cable TV, data or internet plan runs out.

HOW IT WORKS
- Reminders are created automatically after a successful purchase.
- Cable TV: reminder 3 days before expiry, using the date read from the smartcard.

28. BANK TRANSFER (Send to Bank)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
AVAILABILITY: [bankTransferEnabled, default OFF]. While it is off, the Bank Account tab says sending to bank accounts isn't available yet — don't tell customers how to use it. 55. AJO CIRCLE (GROUP CONTRIBUTIONS: AJO / ESUSU / ADASHE)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT IT IS: Save together with people you trust. Every member pays the same amount every day, week or month, and each period one member (by payout number) receives the whole pot. ZAPPI PAY collects and pays out automatically. No interest, no loans — members saving together. [setting: circlesEnabled, default OFF]

BENEFITS
- No chasing people: money is taken from wallets automatically on payment day.
- Everyone sees everything: payout order, who has paid, who is late, the money in the pot.
- The pot is locked: nobody — not members, not the creator, not ZAPPI PAY staff — can spend it before it is paid out.

HOW TO GET STARTED (CREATOR)
1. Home → More → "Ajo Circle" → "Start a circle".
2. Name, amount, how often (daily / weekly / monthly), number of members, start day. Optional: payout fee (max 5% of the pot; 80% to you, 20% to ZAPPI PAY), late fee (max 10% of the contribution), grace hours, missed payments before a payout number moves to the end, extra rules.
3. Accept the agreement and confirm with your PIN.
4. Share the invite link on WhatsApp, or invite people by username. Members join after reading the rules and agreement and confirming with their PIN.
5. When everyone has joined, set the payout order (move people up/down or "Shuffle"), then tap "Start circle".

HOW IT WORKS FOR MEMBERS
- Reminders 3 days before each payment day (weekly/monthly) and a ⚠ low-balance warning the day before.
- On payment day the amount is taken from the wallet. If the balance is short, what's there is taken and the rest is taken as soon as money comes in (it retries every hour).
- Payouts go strictly in order, only when everyone has paid. If someone is late, everyone sees "Payout waiting for: …".
- Late after the grace hours: the late fee (if set) is added — it goes into the pot for that payout — and it counts as a strike.
- Strikes before your payout: after the set number, your payout number moves to the end, automatically.
- Owing after you've been paid: you can't buy, send money or withdraw until it's settled.
- Repeated missed payments: blocked from new circles. Appeal on the Ajo Circle page; ZAPPI PAY decides.
- Need your payout early while someone is late? The member due can ask for the money already collected; they agree the rest follows later, and ZAPPI PAY approves it.
- You can leave before the circle starts, not after. The agreement can be re-read any time on the circle page.

ADMIN: Admin → Ajo Circles: running circles, pots, late payments, early-release requests (approve/reject), appeals, blocked members (unblock), stop a circle (money in unpaid pots goes back to the members who paid it). Switch: Admin → New features (Off / Testers only / On for everyone; OFF until you turn it on). Limits: top of Admin → Ajo Circles. [setting: circleMaxAmount, default ₦500,000] [setting: circleMaxMembers, default 30] [setting: circleBanStrikes, default 3]
